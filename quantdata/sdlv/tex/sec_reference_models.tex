\section{Reference-model implementation details}
\label{app:reference-models}

\subsection{Tangent Levy}
\label{app:tangent-levy}

Our Tangent Levy benchmark follows the codebook construction of
\citet{TangentLevy}, but is an in-repository implementation rather than the
authors' software.  At each observed expiry we fit a double-exponential jump
density to forward-normalized call-equivalent bid--ask intervals.  We then
project the cumulative intensities onto calendar-consistent non-negative
density curves and reconstruct forward L\'evy densities on a daily maturity
grid from zero to 70 business days and a 301-node jump grid on
$[-0.75,0.75]$.  Simulation evolves this codebook using the discrete
convolutional HJM drift and uses the compensated shortest-maturity density for
spot.  The historical diffusion span is the leading 16 constrained PCA
directions, estimated only through 31 December 2024, with 5\% covariance
shrinkage; the audit uses eight substeps per business day.

The initial-fit results in Figures~\ref{fig:initial-fit-iv}
and~\ref{fig:initial-fit-spread} use the final daily-grid reconstruction
supplied to the simulator.  This distinction is important: independent
maturity fits are not a dynamically admissible initial codebook.  The
reconstruction on 29 August 2025 did not pass its pre-specified repricing gate,
so the poor fit shown there is a reported numerical limitation, not suppressed
calibration error.  The
subsequent risk-neutral diagnostics therefore test the implemented projected
Q dynamics conditional on this retained initial state; they do not turn the
failed static reconstruction into a successful market calibration.

\subsection{Matched initial-fit convention}

For the comparison we use the exact 2,662 quotes retained by the GROMM
25-expiry calibration on 29 August 2025.  GROMM is the unrestricted
$25\times200$ state from the
\texttt{direct-continuation-\allowbreak1000-v1} run after
1,000 end-to-end epochs with a 5\% annual Black backbone; this is the same
checkpoint as the direct fit in Table~\ref{tab:numerics-static-market-fit}.
Tangent Levy is repriced at precisely those strikes and expiries
from its daily-grid density.  Put observations are converted to normalized
call-equivalent prices by parity before Black-volatility inversion.  If $b$,
$a$, and $h=(a-b)/2$ are the bid, ask, and half-spread, respectively, the
signed price surface reports
\[
  \widetilde e=
  \begin{cases}
    (C-a)/h,&C>a,\\
    0,&b\leq C\leq a,\\
    (C-b)/h,&C<b,
  \end{cases}
  \qquad e=|\widetilde e|.
\]
The volatility surface applies the same construction after transforming the
bid and ask to Black volatility.  Thus ``quotes with excess'' is the fraction
with $e>0$, and the conditional mean and median are computed only over those
failed quotes.  Both models use the same observations, spreads, axes, and
colour scales.
