# FuNVol research adapter

This isolated module is a **single-asset, public-data adaptation** of Choudhary, Jaimungal, and Bergeron's multi-asset [FuNVol](https://doi.org/10.1080/14697688.2024.2396977). The [authors' code](https://github.com/vedantch/FuNVol) first smooths implied-volatility surfaces on a functional Legendre basis, retains functional principal components, and learns their joint dynamics with equity prices through a GRU neural SDE. It trains separate drift and full-covariance diffusion networks with a Gaussian likelihood and marginal probability-integral-transform (PIT) calibration penalty.

Here the input is solely the distributable SANOS fixed-grid history. We use positive implied volatilities, project inverse-softplus IVs onto a Legendre basis in NMS and square-root expiry, fit FPCs on the chronological training portion, and model FPC scores jointly with cumulative normalized-forward log spot. The one-step return is the increment of that spot coordinate. The returned next IV surface is positive by softplus reconstruction. This is **not** a risk-neutral model: any `Q` use requires a separately validated measure change. Positive reconstructed IVs also do **not** ensure static or dynamic no-arbitrage. The paper reports an empirical static-arbitrage check; this adapter does not make that check an exact guarantee.

`fit_funvol` now accepts an optional dated, epoch-restartable CPU checkpoint. A next-date warm start inherits the preceding neural weights **and the frozen FPC basis and normalization**, so the input and output coordinates do not silently change. It also carries the cumulative log-spot anchor across sliding 252-observation windows. The checkpoint records its as-of date, exact training-data fingerprint, epoch, optimizer and RNG state, and parent date/SHA-256. Inheriting a basis means this is not a rolling FPC estimate; it is a causally fixed coordinate system with rolling neural refits. Any alternative rolling FPC basis would require an explicit weight map. `sample_funvol_one_day` samples one-day spot returns and IV surfaces.

`run_funvol_one_day` and `run_funvol_daily_book` use the common SANOS market marks, fixed-contract hedge book, and ten one-day digitals. They generate one physical-law scenario book, remove simulated instrument means, and fit transaction-cost-aware hedges. The run retains 10,000-path spot, IV, instrument and target scenarios with checkpoint/seed provenance for later objectives. An empirical static-arbitrage screen checks 512 generated surfaces at 11 common forward-normalized strikes within ±0.5% of ATM; it is not a no-arbitrage guarantee. A one-epoch, 45-observation unit-cash pilot on 2023-01-17 produced unstable positions and 10.49% of spot transaction costs, so it is excluded from comparisons. With the 2022-selected 100-epoch model and benchmark ATM digital quantity 0.026007, a separate 10,000-path pilot on that origin had no cap hits and 1.14 basis points of spot costs. The April 1, 2024 pilot initialized directly from 2022 also passed its checks, but was not causally rolling.

The 2022 validation loss selected 100 rather than 300 epochs (3.061 versus 4.113). Subsequent rolling fits set `validation_fraction=0` to use **all information through their as-of date**. The fit-only predecessor chain completed 294 dates from January 2023 through March 2024: 281 fitted checkpoints and 13 documented no-update gaps. Its public SANOS surfaces were precomputed once, but every daily fit received only the causal 252-observation window; direct and cached windows were checked identical. The causal April 1, 2024 fit inherited March 28 weights, then **failed the precommitted spot-dispersion gate**: generated daily spot-return standard deviation was 0.333%, versus 0.778% over the trailing history, below the 0.5 ratio threshold. All ten optimizers converged, there were no cap hits, and the maximum absolute position was 8.68, but no hedge result was published. Its checkpoint, scenario book, exact diagnostic JSON and log remain in the external run directory. Do not relax the gate or report April–May FuNVol MAEs without a newly preselected, independently justified calibration and a full causal rerun. Weekly paths are not implemented.
