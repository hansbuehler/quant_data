# Phase 3 dynamics-estimator comparison

## Conclusion

The DYSANOS/grid matrix-exponential construction is the selected Phase 3
surface dynamics. The implemented hierarchy fits an expiry-causal, lag-only
one-business-session affine transition in training-standardized PCA
coordinates, takes its complete non-symmetric matrix logarithm, and compounds
that generator to the exact five-session Torch step.

The former contemporaneous hierarchy with symmetrized and clipped own-lag
blocks is rejected. Its `0.995` front eigenvalue and 138-week half-life were
created by the projection, not observed in the calibrated volatility data.

## Frozen experiment

- State source: completed market-to-Strong encoder history.
- Drift/PCA fit: 2022-01-03 through 2024-12-31 only.
- Validation: 2025-01-02 through 2025-08-29 only.
- No 2026 observations are read.
- State geometry: Strong `140`, with per-expiry 99.5% PCA giving reduced sizes
  `(8,13,13,12,12,10,10,10,5,5,5,5,5,5)` and total dimension `118`.
- Drift observations: 675 exact adjacent NYSE-session pairs.
- Weekly innovation observations: 671 exact five-session pairs.
- Every date origin has equal weight; option count does not weight a date.

All comparison fits standardize every retained PCA coordinate using training
means and population standard deviations. Reported preliminary RMSE and
stationary trace ratios are therefore in this common standardized metric.

principal matrix logarithm is materially complex: maximum imaginary component

weekly full-log generator is a failed estimator for the whole surface. The
front-only exact logarithm is real and stable. The Euler full-K remains a valid
control, but the daily DYSANOS/grid estimator forecasts slightly better.

## Hierarchical test

For expiry block `j`, the fitted one-session equation is

\[
 z_{t+1}^{j}
   = c_j + z_t^j B_{jj} +
     \mathbf 1_{j>1}z_t^{j-1}B_{j-1,j}+e_{t+1}^j.
\]

There is no `z_{t+1}^{j-1}` regressor. Assemble the block-upper-bidiagonal
daily transition `B`, require its complete principal logarithm to be real and
stable, and define

\[
 K_d=-\log B,
 \qquad A_{1W}=\exp(-5K_d)=B^5.
\]

Although `B` is adjacent-expiry, `K_d` and `A_{1W}` contain the transitive
earlier-expiry effects generated by matrix logarithm/exponentiation. They
remain expiry-causal and non-diagonal. No coefficient scaling, symmetrization,
eigenvalue clipping, or silent fallback is applied.

The chosen exact-log hierarchy produced:

| reduced-118 statistic | result |
|---|---:|
| daily-log maximum imaginary component | 0 |
| longest whole-surface half-life | 3.69 weeks |
| longest front half-life | 3.69 weeks |
| weekly spectral radius | 0.82869 |
| weekly standardized norm | 1.25697 |
| maximum standardized `||A^n||`, `n<=1040` | 1.25708 at week 2 |
| `||A^52||` | `1.62e-4` |
| `||A^260||` | `1.72e-21` |
| stationary/training variance trace | 0.94824 |
| raw-coordinate transition condition | 3,042.76 |
| equilibrium distance in standardized coordinates | 0.457 training RMS |

The unrestricted full weekly innovation covariance is retained. This replaces
the former independent-expiry residual assumption, which was contradicted by
cross-expiry residual correlations.

## Remaining research limitations

- The hierarchy is a statistical expiry-causal scaffold, not by itself an HJM
  no-arbitrage restriction.
- Full mode forecasts better than reduced mode, while reduced mode is smaller
  and much better conditioned. Both are retained for research.
- Gaussian innovations still require tail-model research.
- The market encoder's 2025 state/quote-fit deterioration remains upstream of
  the dynamics and is not repaired by this estimator.
- Any future measure change must act on the assembled exact weekly transition,
  not on daily PCA shocks.
