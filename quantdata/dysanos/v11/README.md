# DYSANOS 1.1

This directory isolates changes made after the model documented in
`quantdata/dysanos/tex/`. The published-paper implementation and results are
not modified in place.

The first experiment is a strict rolling-origin comparison with a 252-session
lookback and warm starts between adjacent origins:

1. fit the current DYSANOS fixed-grid panel;
2. at each origin, define a richer GROMM-style grid and reprice that grid on
   each of the preceding fitted SANOS surfaces;
3. compare forward fit and generated spot/rolling-ATM-volatility correlation;
4. fit the AR(1) law and the tail-law extensions only after selecting the
   observation geometry.

The historical market quote grids may be irregular. SANOS supplies the
arbitrage-free interpolation used to construct each origin's rectangular
lookback panel. No surface dated after the rolling origin may enter that
panel.

The spot return and state innovation must be fitted jointly. Validation reports
the correlation between the one-session spot return and changes in rolling ATM
volatility at every model tenor; fitting only a latent-state/spot copula is not
sufficient. Because each rolling affine fit has a gauge freedom, overlapping
warm-started latent histories must also be aligned before terminal states are
combined into the series used by the AR(1) and tail-law fits.

`atm_spot_correlation.py` is the reproducible baseline diagnostic for the
published all-history checkpoint. It distinguishes the direct historical SANOS
term structure, its affine reconstruction and paths generated by the fitted
dynamics.

Generated panels, checkpoints, logs and reports belong under unique run
directories in the configured external data root, not in this source tree.

## Current model disposition

The current selected dynamics benchmark is the **daily joint Direct-200 model
with Gaussian innovations**.  Its daily state/spot construction materially
improves independent 2025 predictive calibration relative to the weekly law.

The following are retained research comparators, not selected production
models:

- daily and weekly Tail Diffusion laws, which fit heavier innovation-radius
  tails but whose current stationary-scale daily specification is
  over-dispersed on the 2025 forward holdout;
- LSTM and selective-SSM conditional laws, which improve conditional NLL but
  have not shown a material improvement in the targeted multistep path
  statistics;
- the pre-2025 weekly Direct-200 fit, which remains the paper-compatible
  baseline and source of the weekly-comparison reports.

Do not extend the direct daily-spot MLP pilot, either the regularised or the
rolling decoded DLV/backbone spot-loading correction, or the DLV-only
experiment.  They are retained as diagnostics: the first did not generalize;
the spot-loading corrections improved their local feature regression but not
the output leverage term structure; and the third established that the fixed
Black backbone is not the sole source of the leverage shortfall.  Retiring a
line means no new runs or checkpoint deletion; its reproducible report
remains part of the research record.
